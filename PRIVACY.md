# Privacy Policy – DatoCrack

DatoCrack is a personal tool used only by its owner.

- **Data collected:** only the OAuth access token and the basic account ID (open_id) returned by TikTok, needed to upload videos to the owner's own account.
- **Storage:** tokens are stored privately and are never published or shared.
- **Third parties:** no data is sold or shared with anyone.
- **Other users:** the app does not access data from any other TikTok user.
- **Deletion:** the owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Last updated: 16 November 2026.
